Connect this browser

COS runs on your Mac. Sign in once and this browser stays connected for 7 days.

In COS Control on your Mac, choose Open COS on your phone. Scan its QR with the camera, or tap Show a sign-in code and type it here. A browser that is already signed in can also make one: Settings, Sign in another device.

The glasses pairing code does not work here.

In the COS menu-bar panel on your Mac, choose Copy pairing token. The copy clears after 60 seconds, so paste within a minute.
